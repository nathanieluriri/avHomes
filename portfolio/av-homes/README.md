# AV Homes: portfolio videos

Five silent, seamlessly looping showcase videos of the AV Homes product, for the AV Homes entry of
the portfolio. Each is 16 to 18 s at 1920x1080, 60 fps, made from real screens of the product run
locally with demo data (every private detail is demo data; money totals carry a "Demo data" label).

| Slug | Title | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| `av-homes` (cover) | AV Homes | A buyer asks about a listing on the site; the enquiry lands in the console |
| `av-homes-mail` | AV Homes Mailboxes | The team's Hostinger mailboxes in the console: read, reply, sent |
| `av-homes-marketer-app` | AV Homes Marketers | The marketer phone app: report a sale, the 5% commission, pay day |
| `av-homes-partner` | AV Homes Partners | A developer applies, is approved, and sends a first estate for review |
| `av-homes-money` | AV Homes Money | Approve a commission split, both funds grow, pay day marked paid |

Each folder holds `<slug>.mp4` (H.264, 1440x810), `<slug>.webm` (VP9), `<slug>.webp` (poster),
`portfolio-entry.json` (title, wheel words, description, stack, colors, duration), `STORYBOARD.md`
and `SOURCES.md` (where every string, number and route on screen comes from). `manifest.json` lists
all five in display order, with paths relative to this folder.
