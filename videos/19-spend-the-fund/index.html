Spending the community fund

A share of every deal goes in. Pay some out, and leave a receipt behind it.

Step 1 of 8
The two funds
A share of every recorded deal goes into each, whoever closed it and whether or not anybody earned commission.
Step 2 of 8
What the Foundation holds
Held now, all time. A balance is the sum of its history, never a stored number.
Step 3 of 8
Record a payment out
This is the only way money leaves it. The prize pool is different: that leaves through an award.
Step 4 of 8
How much
It will not take more than the fund holds. A negative balance would be the fund claiming it gave away money it never had.
Step 5 of 8
What it paid for
Write what a reader needs a year from now. Not "project".
Step 6 of 8
The receipt
At least one. Money leaving needs evidence, the same rule a recorded sale follows.
Step 7 of 8
Record it
The balance drops by exactly that amount.
Step 8 of 8
And the history says why
The payment sits at the top, carrying what it paid for and who recorded it. Nothing here is ever edited.
